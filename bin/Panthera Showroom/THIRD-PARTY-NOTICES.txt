The original package metadata classifies this package as “Freeware w/sources”.

LICENSE or COPYING text; the metadata points to a general Wikipedia page.
Redistribution permission is unresolved. DOSLFN and CP437UNI.TBL are not
included with Panthera Works. The current applications retain LFN-dependent

was made. Native LFN implementations are outside the scope of this release.
